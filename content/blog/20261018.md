I got a few things accomplished this weekend. The first was a long-ish drive in my Buick. It was about 80 degrees outside on Friday so I took it for the longest drive I've ever done in it, about 10 miles. The car ran fine but seemed to sputter a little at idle. At speed, though, it ran great. I think I still have an ignition problem or a slight miss. I also had a power steering leak that sprayed fluid all over the engine bay. Short-term goals for the car are:
- Re-set dwell, idle rpm, idle mixture, and timing
- Run 1/4" return line to the fuel tank and install a return-style fuel pressure regulator
- Run a can of Berryman's B12 through the gas tank

I also made progress on a window replacement that I did last week. Of course, removing the old trim, which had bare, unpainted 1920s plaster underneath, pulled away paint from the wall. So now I'm skim coating plaster, sanding, and painting before I install new trim.

Another cool thing I did this weekend was cook agnolotti, a fresh filled pasta similar to ravioli. I used a food processor to make the pasta dough which turned out great. 

Long-term goals:

- Finish and "release" an EP of 4 previously unrecorded songs
- Look into other career-related opportunities...maybe teach a class?
