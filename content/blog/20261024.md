I took another afternoon to run the Buick and get things dialed in. Fuel pressure, dwell, timing, etc. were all spot on. The car drives great but it seems to sputter a little or miss when it's warm. I checked the coil and found that it was slightly out of spec so I swapped in a spare. I feel like the fuel is getting hot and the regulator isn't helping. I'm also running a can of Berryman's through the gas tank. I checked plugs and they are (finally) not fouled and look normal. I think this might just be how it is until I can get a return line run to the fuel tank. Oh, and I also need to reseal the power steering pump, which is spraying fluid under the hood when I drive.
I'm planning to finishing trimming and painting the new window I installed earlier this month.
