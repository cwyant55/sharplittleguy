I had a few days off work this week. One of my main goals this season is to get my Buick drivable without any leaks, etc. Aside from all the issues with this car that I've described earlier, I've had a peristent, minor coolant leak near the thermostat housing. I also discovered a power steering leak after I started driving the car more than a few miles. This week, I was able to pull the power steering pump and install a new shaft seal. I also pulled the thermostat housing/water neck and installed a new one, with a new gasket and gasket sealer. The old water neck was pitted on the mating surface. This seems to have fixed all of the issues, but I suppose time will tell. The car is also running a little smoother, maybe? Maybe it's the Berryman's, or maybe it's just me.
I also took the Plymouth on a nice 20 mile cruise to blow the cobwebs out. That car just runs great and starts up every time. Long-term, like next summer, I'd like to drive the Buick regularly and do some floor patching on the Plymouth.
